## Meta Description — Rejected & Alternative Drafts

**Current in `index.html`:**
> Beyond mind maps. Knogra is an AI-powered knowledge graph editor — map ideas spatially, discover hidden connections, and explore any topic with an AI that understands your entire map.

*Problem: "map" used 3x; "discover hidden connections" / "explore any topic" too generic.*

**Alternative A (concrete, technical):**
> Beyond mind maps. Knogra is a knowledge graph editor with topology-aware AI — guided tours through complex topics, long-distance associations across domains, and a design system that emerges as you think.

**Alternative B (provocative, contrastive):**
> Mind maps are sketches. Outlines are prisons. Knogra is a knowledge graph with a built-in narrator — scenes, paths, and a topology-aware AI that reads your structure, not just your text.

**Alternative C (rhythmic triplets):**
> Beyond mind maps. Beyond outlines. Knogra turns thinking into a navigable atlas — with topology-aware AI, cinematic scenes, and a design that emerges as you work.

## Original Deep Analysis (from `dev-notes.md`)

### Three Friction Points
1. **The Hierarchy Prison** — folders force top-down structure; insights happen at the periphery
2. **The Blind AI Problem** — text-only AI can't read spatial structure
3. **The Death-by-PowerPoint Gap** — interconnected thinking → linear slides loses everything

### Three Positioning Pillars
1. **Presentation layer for complex systems** — "Stop flattening your thoughts into slides"
2. **Sovereign by design** — "Your research. Your data. Your API key."
3. **Topology-aware AI** — "An AI that understands structure, not just text"

### Added by us
4. **The Design Tax** — visual richness emerges as you think, not as separate design step

## Language Notes

**Avoid (bland/overused in 2026):**
- "AI-powered" *(everything is)*
- "discover hidden connections" *(generic)*
- "explore any topic" *(empty verb)*
- "unleash your creativity" / "supercharge your..."

**Prefer (specific, vivid):**
- topology-aware *(precise)*
- spatial / atlas / living map *(geographic metaphors)*
- camera crew / cinematic / narrator *(presentation metaphors)*
- long-distance associations *(concrete with examples)*
- emerges naturally *(captures the no-design-tax idea)*
