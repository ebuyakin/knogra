Features

Scenes

A large graph is hard to navigate all at once — so Knogra lets you break it into focused views, each scoped to what matters right now. They all draw from one connected graph. Complexity stays manageable no matter how far your knowledge grows.

Transitions

Moving between scenes is animated — shared ideas drift to their new positions rather than the screen jumping to something unrelated. Your brain encodes knowledge spatially: you remember a concept by where it sat and how you reached it. Knogra turns navigation into memory.

Topology-Aware AI

Chat with an assistant that understands your graph's structure — which ideas are well-connected, which are isolated, and what's missing. It suggests what to add next; you accept with one click.

No Design Tax

Your graph looks good without extra effort. Styles, themes, and backgrounds take shape as you build — picking a look for an idea is part of the thinking, not a separate cleanup step. 20+ node designs, multiple themes, custom backgrounds.

Paths

Build a guided walk through your graph — for teaching, presenting, or studying. Take someone through a complex topic step by step, without losing the connections that make it meaningful.

LaTeX Support

First-class math rendering inside nodes. Equations are knowledge too.

Your Data, Your Rules

Everything stays in your browser. No server, no account, no tracking.

IndexedDB storage. Works offline. Export and import anytime.

Bring your own AI API key — calls go directly to your chosen provider.
