Privacy Policy

Effective May 2026

The short version

Knogra does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. Everything you create stays in your browser.

App data

All graph data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to Knogra servers. Knogra has no backend database.

You can export your data at any time from the app. You own everything you create.

Analytics

The landing page (knogra.io) uses Vercel Web Analytics. This collects anonymous, aggregate data — page views and referrers. No personal data, no cookies, no fingerprinting.

The app (/app/) does not include any analytics.

AI features

If you use AI features, you supply your own API key. Knogra supports the Google Gemini API and OpenRouter (which routes requests to a range of AI models). Requests are sent directly from your browser to Google or OpenRouter. Knogra never receives or processes these requests. The privacy policies of Google and OpenRouter apply to any data sent during those calls.

Cookies

Knogra does not use tracking cookies. The app uses browser localStorage and IndexedDB to store your settings and graph data locally.

Third parties

The only third-party service active on the landing page is Vercel Web Analytics. No advertising networks, no social tracking pixels.

Contact

Questions? Email contact@knogra.io.
